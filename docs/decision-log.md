## Interpretation notes

### R16 Core-backed super admin (ADM-048)

Signed off by the lead on 2026-10-14 (R15/R16 full spec, lead question 16). Sources: the lead's Q-L4 and Q-L9, the owner's answers OQ14–OQ23, D27 §3, §4 and §11, and the build spec `sr-admin-0083-0084.md`.

**What ADM-048 lifts, for R16 only:**

- **ADM-041 (no scope growth).** The R16 pages may be added or re-pointed:
  - Overview, Businesses, a Business (Summary, then Records behind a reason sheet, with a 30-minute banner);
  - Flags, Access log;
  - Associations for the HOA level (0086, D18): the list with each one's kind (HOA or barangay, the owner's D18 answers), creating one after checking its letter, adding or ending an officer, rotating the join code, closing. Until Core has 0086 the page says what it will do.
- **ADM-044**, in part:
  - **Supabase production adapters:** a Core adapter behind `AdminService` that POSTs PostgREST RPCs with the signed-in administrator's `aal2` token;
  - **production financial visibility:** behind a typed reason at Summary level, and at Records level only behind an open flag or a support or legal reference (break-glass), for 30 minutes at a time;
  - **production deployment** of this site;
  - **durable production mutations, only for the administrator's own records:** opening and closing an access, and raising and reviewing a flag. These write Core's admin log, never a business's records. With 0086, also the association records (create, officers, join code, close; D18 §2), never a business's records (spec §11 Q8).
- **ADM-047 (no deploy).** `admin.kitamo.online` may be deployed for R16 as its own Vercel project. The owner's DNS and Turnstile host steps come first (STEP5 §4).
- **ADM-043, interpreted for this slice.** The Core RPCs' allowlisted reply keys, parsed with strict zod schemas that fail closed on an unknown key, are this slice's machine-readable contract. Wider shared contracts stay with the Shared Contracts workstream.

**What stays in force:**

- **ADM-012, ADM-013, ADM-021 and ADM-022:** pages use `AdminService` only; the mock and Core adapters share contract tests; reads come first, and R16 is read-only toward businesses (OQ21).
- **ADM-016, ADM-024 and ADM-025:** mock roles and scenario tools never reach a production build.
- **ADM-019:** account suspension and restoration stay disabled. Every account action stays parked (D27 §12).
- **ADM-020:** whole merchant transaction data stays out of the default views. Only the break-glass Records level shows records, read-only, each read logged and disclosed to the business (OQ16, OQ17).
- **ADM-044's Clerk part:** Clerk is dropped from the plan, not adopted; the identity source is KitaMo's Auth. The service-role adapter is dropped too: no service-role key exists in this repo or its bundle, and `scripts/check-client-secrets.mjs` and `check-production-bundle.mjs` enforce it.
- **The repo's three roles** stay in its matrix. R16 uses only `super_admin`, held by at most one live Core `platform_admins` row (the owner's separate account with no business, OQ14).
- **The Android-era pages** (Devices, Synchronization, App versions) stay parked until R23 and are hidden in production.

**Conditions of the lift:**

1. **The gate is Core's.** Every read goes through a DEFINER function that calls `km_admin_can` first, and each read is logged (the AGENTS.md line the lead adds at R16's build start, D27 §2). The site's own checks only route the user; they never authorize data.
2. **The token must be `aal2`.** It is ES256-verified with the vendored KitaMo sign-in check (D19 §5, copied from Operations at dbe4ca6). Anything else goes to MFA or sign-in.
3. **The `admin` switch ships OFF.** The lead turns it on after the lawyer's review of the privacy wording (Q-L9). While it is off, the site shows its maintenance page.
4. **Every access is disclosed** to the business in KitaMo ("Checked by KitaMo"). The only exception is a law-required hold of at most 90 days, logged (OQ17).
5. **Flags are leads, not proof.** They are never shown to the seller and never acted on alone (OQ18).
6. **The SQL-editor rule (OQ22):** before the operator reads or fixes one business's records in the database tool, an access with a reason is opened on this site.

**Expiry.** ADM-048 covers the R16 slice only. Any later scope, including more administrators, any write to a business, account actions, LGU or enterprise levels, or Android-era pages, needs a new entry.

### Shared Data Contract Baseline v0.1

The approved **KitaMo Shared Data Contract Baseline v0.1** is the cross-system semantic authority for `kitamo-ph/owner-seller-mobile`, `platform-api`, `kitamo-ph/admin`, and the future `kitamo-ph/shared-contracts` workstream. This is an interpretation of the existing ADM decisions, especially ADM-012 through ADM-015 and ADM-021 through ADM-023; it does not create or change an ADM decision ID or status.

For Admin Platform work, v0.1 governs:

- `stall` as the canonical term outside legacy Android SQLite, with any `branch` compatibility mapping kept explicit;
- stable opaque identifiers, preservation of legacy IDs, and an approved globally collision-resistant format for new cloud-capable records;
- UTC storage for actual instants, explicit IANA business timezones, and business-date interpretation in the business timezone;
- currency codes, integer minor units for posted money where practical, at least six-decimal precision for rates/intermediate costing, and centralized/versioned final rounding;
- synchronization meanings, correlation context, and contract compatibility; generic processed-record counts are not merchant transaction counts;
- append-only canonical audit requirements for privileged, exceptional, and financially corrective actions; and
- separate `contract_version` and physical `schema_version` meanings and compatibility gates.

Local TypeScript/Zod schemas are executable, least-privilege Admin projection validators pinned to v0.1. They are not an independent cross-system source of truth and do not authorize the Admin Platform to redefine Android accounting, product, inventory, sales, refund, or synchronization semantics.

The baseline resolves canonical terminology and semantics but does not supply the physical production artifacts that remain gated: Android compatibility mappings and machine-readable shared contracts, Supabase schema/migrations/functions/grants/RLS, Clerk-to-Supabase identity mapping, the authoritative administrator-assignment source, or durable audit storage/retention controls. Missing or conflicting artifacts still stop the affected production integration slice; no local projection may invent them.

### Process-local mutations

ADM-018 and ADM-023 are implemented as a single mock service use case: a permitted problem-report update creates a corresponding in-memory audit entry. Neither record is durable across restarts or shared serverless instances. A production adapter requires transactional persistence based on the inspected schema.

### Preview versus production

ADM-016 and ADM-024 permit role/scenario/delay/reset tools only in local or explicitly designated preview environments. ADM-025 requires build- and server-level exclusion from production. The current instruction is no deployment, so preview and target-domain setup remain future recommendations.
